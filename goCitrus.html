GoCitrus App

Summary

GoCitrus is an event tracker app that focuses on finding events specifically in Orlando, Florida. It acts as a management tool that can find local or big events near you, save specific functions, and buy tickets from within the app itself. It is designed with everyone in mind, whether they are a tourist navigating through Orlando for the first time, a college student looking for free events, or a parent wanting to plan a vacation for their family.
